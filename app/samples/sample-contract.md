# Sample sale contract — test fixture

Paste the text below the rule into **Project & programme → Contract → the text box**,
then press Index. It is written to exercise every field `index-contract` extracts:
signing date, parties, unit, payment, delivery, variation and warranty clauses, the
total price, and a five-instalment payment schedule.

The instalment milestones are worded to match the activities in `sample-week1.xml`
("Foundation concrete pour", "Structure complete", "Stone cladding", "Handover"), so
the Money screen lines up against the imported programme.

This is a **test fixture**, not a legal document. A real sale in Lebanon is notarised
and should be drafted by a lawyer.

---

CONTRACT OF SALE OF A RESIDENTIAL UNIT UNDER CONSTRUCTION

Signed on 12 January 2026 at Beirut.

BETWEEN:

PMCC S.A.R.L., a Lebanese limited liability company registered in Beirut under
commercial register number 1996/4412, with offices at Beirut Central District,
Beirut, Lebanon, represented by its General Manager, hereinafter "the Seller";

AND:

Mr. Rami Khoury, holder of Lebanese identity card number 1978/03311, residing at
Achrafieh, Beirut, Lebanon, hereinafter "the Purchaser".

ARTICLE 1 — THE UNIT

The Seller sells to the Purchaser the residence known as Residence 2, situated on the
first floor of the building erected on plot 563, Daher El Souane, Metn, Lebanon. The
residence comprises a built area of 245 square metres together with a private garden
of 90 square metres, two covered parking bays numbered P4 and P5, and one storage
room numbered S3 in the basement. The sale includes the finishes described in the
specification schedule annexed to this contract and initialled by both parties. The
common parts of the building are held in undivided co-ownership in proportion to the
Purchaser's share.

ARTICLE 2 — PRICE

The total price of the residence is four hundred and eighty thousand United States
Dollars (USD 480,000), payable in the instalments set out in Article 3. The price is
firm and is not subject to indexation.

ARTICLE 3 — PAYMENT SCHEDULE

The Purchaser shall pay the price as follows:

1. On signature of this contract: thirty percent (30%) of the price, being
   USD 144,000.
2. On completion of the foundation concrete pour: twenty percent (20%) of the price,
   being USD 96,000.
3. On structure complete: twenty percent (20%) of the price, being USD 96,000.
4. On completion of the stone cladding to the external elevations: fifteen percent
   (15%) of the price, being USD 72,000.
5. On handover of the residence: fifteen percent (15%) of the price, being
   USD 72,000.

Each instalment falls due within fifteen (15) days of the Seller notifying the
Purchaser in writing that the corresponding stage has been reached. No instalment is
due before its stage has been built.

ARTICLE 4 — DELIVERY

The Seller undertakes to deliver the residence to the Purchaser on or before
24 November 2026. A grace period of ninety (90) days beyond that date is allowed and
does not constitute delay. Should delivery not occur within the grace period, and
save for events of force majeure or delay caused by the Purchaser, the Seller shall
pay the Purchaser compensation of USD 1,500 for each completed month of delay,
capped at five percent (5%) of the price.

ARTICLE 5 — VARIATIONS

No variation to the specification shall be carried out unless recorded in a written
variation order stating its price and its effect on the delivery date, and signed by
both parties before the work is executed. An approved variation adjusts the price and,
where it states one, the delivery date. Work carried out without a signed variation
order is at the Seller's cost and does not extend the delivery date.

ARTICLE 6 — WARRANTY

The Seller warrants the structure of the building, including its foundations, frame
and waterproofing, for ten (10) years from the date of handover. Finishes, joinery,
sanitary ware and mechanical and electrical installations are warranted for one (1)
year from the date of handover. The warranty does not cover normal wear, damage
caused by the Purchaser, or alterations carried out by third parties.

ARTICLE 7 — GOVERNING LAW

This contract is governed by Lebanese law. Any dispute arising from it shall be
submitted to the competent courts of Beirut.

Signed in two original copies, each party having received one.
